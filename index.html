Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about insurance coverage or the procedure? Find answers below.

1. Will health insurance cover my Westchester breast reduction surgery?

Many insurance providers cover reduction mammoplasty if it is deemed medically necessary to alleviate chronic physical pain (such as back, neck, or shoulder pain). Our team helps review your specific policy guidelines and documentation.

2. What medical criteria do insurance companies look for?

Insurers typically look for documented documentation of chronic symptoms, physical therapy or chiropractic records, rashes or skin breakdown under the breast crease, and often a specific minimum weight of breast tissue (Schnur scale) to be removed.

3. How long does the insurance pre-authorization process take?

The pre-authorization timeline varies depending on your insurance provider, usually taking anywhere from 2 to 6 weeks once all required medical records and physician letters are submitted.

4. What is the recovery time after a reduction mammoplasty?

Most patients can return to light desk work and normal daily routines within 1 to 2 weeks, though strenuous physical activity and heavy lifting should be avoided for about 4 to 6 weeks.
